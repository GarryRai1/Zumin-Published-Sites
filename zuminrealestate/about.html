Our Story

Serving Ontario since 2014.

Zumin Real Estate Centre has been serving Ontario since 2014. We built Realli® to connect every professional in a real estate transaction — agents, mortgage brokers, contractors, lawyers, and insurers — in one platform.

Our mission is simple: make real estate less fragmented, less stressful, and more rewarding for everyone involved. From buying your first home to selling your family estate, Zumin brings the expertise, technology, and people together.
